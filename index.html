Method

How I evaluate a response

01

Read the rubric first

Every task starts with the guidelines, not the responses — so scoring stays consistent across a whole batch.

02

Compare systematically

Responses are checked line by line for factual accuracy, completeness, and tone before any ranking is made.

03

Flag with evidence

Every flag — hallucination, bias, unsafe content — comes with a short, specific written rationale.

04

Calibrate and repeat

Scores are periodically re-checked against earlier tasks in the same batch to catch drift.
